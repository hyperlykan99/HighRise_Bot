1. ADD !RADIOACHIEVEMENTS
==================================================

Add command:

Aliases if safe:

Shows the user’s radio achievement progress.

Output must be compact and under 249 chars per message.
Use pagination if needed.

Example:

==================================================
2. ACHIEVEMENT TIERS
==================================================

Use hard milestone tiers.

🎧 Radio Fan
Based on successful requests:
- Bronze: 25 requests
- Silver: 100 requests
- Gold: 250 requests
- Legend: 500 requests

⭐ Taste Maker
Based on likes given:
- Bronze: 25 likes
- Silver: 100 likes
- Gold: 300 likes
- Legend: 750 likes

🔥 Hit Maker
Based on the most-liked song requested by the user:
- Bronze: one requested song gets 10 likes
- Silver: one requested song gets 25 likes
- Gold: one requested song gets 50 likes
- Legend: one requested song gets 100 likes

📂 Playlist Curator
Based on playlists and playlist songs:
- Bronze: 3 playlists + 25 playlist songs
- Silver: 5 playlists + 100 playlist songs
- Gold: 10 playlists + 250 playlist songs
- Legend: 500 playlist songs

💿 Collector
Based on favorites:
- Bronze: 25 favorites
- Silver: 100 favorites
- Gold: 250 favorites
- Legend: 500 favorites

🏆 Radio Legend
Ultimate achievement.
Requires all:
- 500 successful requests
- 500 likes/favorites combined
- 10 playlists
- one requested song with 100 likes

==================================================
3. DATA SOURCES
==================================================

Use existing sources:

Requests:
- radio_user_stats.requests_count
- fallback: yt_request_jobs successful request count

Likes given:
- radio_user_stats.likes_count
- fallback: dj_ratings where user_id and rating='like'

Favorites:
- radio_user_stats.favorites_count
- fallback: dj_favorites count

Playlists:
- radio_user_stats.playlists_created
- fallback: radio_playlists count

Playlist songs:
- radio_user_stats.playlist_songs_added
- fallback: radio_playlist_songs count

Hit Maker:
Use requested songs by this user and count likes on those songs.
Possible sources:
- yt_request_jobs title/song_key/user_id
- radio_song_stats like_count
- dj_ratings aggregated by song_key

If Hit Maker cannot be computed safely yet:
show:
🔥 Hit Maker: Tracking soon
but do not crash.
