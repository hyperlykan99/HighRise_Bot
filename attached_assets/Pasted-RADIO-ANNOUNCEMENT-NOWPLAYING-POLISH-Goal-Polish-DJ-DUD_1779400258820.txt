==================================================
1. FIX DJ ANNOUNCEMENT LIKES/DISLIKES
==================================================

Bug:
DJ announcement displays likes/dislikes as 0 even when songs have ratings.

Fix:
DJ announcements must read real likes/dislikes from the same rating source used by:
- !like
- !dislike
- !ratings

Do not use a separate rating counter.
Do not default to 0 if ratings exist.

Use stable song key:
- YouTube URL or video ID if available
- else AzuraCast song unique_id if available
- else normalized title + artist

Announcement should show:
👍 <real_like_count> | 👎 <real_dislike_count>

==================================================
2. KEEP VIRTUAL MEDIA PLAYER DESIGN
==================================================

Never remove the virtual media player design.

DJ announcement and !np / !nowplaying must keep:
- separated title
- separated artist
- elapsed time
- progress bar
- total duration

Required format:

🎧 DJ DUDU RADIO
▶️ Now Playing

🎵 Title: <title>
🎤 Artist: <artist>

<elapsed> <progress_bar> <duration>

👍 <likes> | 👎 <dislikes>
💿 Request: !play <song or YouTube URL>

Example:

🎧 DJ DUDU RADIO
▶️ Now Playing

🎵 Title: Folded
🎤 Artist: Kehlani

1:20 ▰▰▰▰▱▱▱▱▱▱ 4:20

👍 12 | 👎 2
💿 Request: !play <song or YouTube URL>

If artist is missing:
🎤 Artist: Unknown Artist

If duration is missing:
Use a safe fallback:
0:00 ▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱▱ ?:??

Do not remove the progress bar.
