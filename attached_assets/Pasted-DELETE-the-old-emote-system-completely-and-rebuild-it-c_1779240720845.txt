!unresolvedplayeremotes

Commands:

!emotes [page]
- Shows BOT_SELF_EMOTES.

!playeremotes [page]
- Shows resolved PLAYER_EMOTES only.

!unresolvedplayeremotes
- Shows PLAYER_EMOTE_NAMES that could not map to an ID.

!emoteid <name>
- Shows bot/self raw ID.

!playeremoteid <name>
- Shows player raw ID.

!botemote @botname <name>
- Uses BOT_SELF_EMOTES.
- Sends exact raw ID with send_emote(emote_id).

If player types:
heartfingers
- Resolve from PLAYER_EMOTES.
- Send exact raw ID to that player:
  await bot.highrise.send_emote(emote_id, user.id)

If player types an unresolved name, ignore silently.

Do not invent missing IDs.
Do not guess missing player emotes.
Do not auto-prefix anything.
